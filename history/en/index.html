MIURA A-TEI

Miura History Walk

A Yayoi village, Yoritomo's three villas, the last stand of the Miura clan, beacons and lighthouses, “Rain on Jogashima” and the tuna port. 29 historic places in Miura, numbered from oldest to newest, on a map where the land rises in 3D. Pin colours show the era. Place names on the map are partly in Japanese.

“Tour the eras” flies over the places from the Yayoi period to Showa, oldest first (touch the map to stop). Numbers match the stories below. White pins with a number group nearby places (tap to spread them). Use the arrow buttons at the lower left to rotate and tilt. Land and building heights are true to scale. Buildings without recorded heights are shown at 5 m.

Everything here was checked against the sources listed under each item (mostly in Japanese). 🟢 = checked on a Miura City or Miura Tourism Association page; 🟡 = from a secondary source such as Wikipedia. “Legend” marks stories that include tradition. Pin positions come from OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia and may be off by tens of metres. Temples, shrines and research facilities have their own opening hours and access rules. Translated from the Japanese; if they differ, the Japanese version is correct.
