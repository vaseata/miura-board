Where the flowers bloom and the views open up around Misaki, Jogashima and Miura Kaigan, month by month. Diamond Fuji from Jogashima Bridge is listed all year.

Diamond FujiTwice a year

FROM JOGASHIMA BRIDGE · AT SUNSET

Twice a year, the sun sets right on the summit of Mt. Fuji

around May 1 (Sat)in 168 days
around Aug 11 (Wed)in 270 days

Seen at sunset around May 1 and around August 11 each year from the middle of Jogashima Bridge (dates shift a little by year). Mt. Fuji is about 85 km away to the west-northwest. The bridge is about 23 m above the sea, with fishing ports on both sides and Fuji beyond the water.

Cormorants of Jogashima

BestNov–Apr

In winter, Japanese cormorants, pelagic cormorants and Pacific reef herons come to Jogashima. Their roost on the cliffs east of Akabanezaki is a prefectural natural monument; the cliffs can’t be approached, so watch from a distance.

ViewMisaki–Jogashima

Jogashima Bridge

You can walk across this 575 m bridge. Nothing blocks the view: the Boso Peninsula to the east, Izu Oshima to the south and Mt. Fuji to the west. Diamond Fuji is seen from here too.

ViewJogashima (west)

Jogashima Lighthouse

Japan’s fifth Western-style lighthouse, first lit in 1870 to a design by the French engineer Verny. Great views from the white tower on the cliff, with souvenir shops and seafood restaurants below. About 5 minutes’ walk from the Jogashima bus stop.

ViewJogashima (east tip)

Awasaki Lighthouse (the radish lighthouse)

A lighthouse shaped like an upside-down Miura radish, shading to green at the bottom. It was built on this spot in March 2020. About 10 minutes’ walk from the Hakushuhi-mae bus stop.

ViewJogashima (south)

Umanose rock arch

A rock arch carved by waves over many years. The top is closed because it may collapse. The path is steep, so wear good shoes. About 15 minutes’ walk from the Hakushuhi-mae bus stop.

Kurosaki-no-Hana

A path through tall grass suddenly opens onto the sea, with Mt. Fuji in the distance and no buildings in sight. You can explore the rock pools in a small cove. No parking or toilets. About 18 minutes’ walk from the Miyata bus stop.

FlowersJogashima

Double daffodils of Jogashima

BestJan–Feb · mid-January to mid-February; daffodil festival in February

About 300,000 double daffodils fill Jogashima Park and the “Daffodil Road” from the park to Jogashima Lighthouse. They also line the path from the park entrance to Awasaki Lighthouse at the eastern tip.

FlowersMiura Kaigan

Kawazu cherry and rapeseed blossoms, Miura Kaigan

BestFeb–Mar · Cherry festival: Feb 5 – Mar 3, 2027

About 1,000 Kawazu cherry trees line the Keikyu tracks for about 1 km from Miurakaigan Station to Komatsugaike Park, with rapeseed blossoms in bloom at the same time.

TipParking is scarce and roads get busy, so come by train
