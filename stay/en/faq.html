Before you come

What time can I check in? What if I arrive early?

14:00–21:00. You cannot enter outside these hours. If you want to drop off luggage early, you need the house manager's OK in advance.

More →

Which bus stop: Hinode or Misaki Higashioka?

Hinode (日の出) is best. It is close to the house and the road is almost flat. From Misaki Higashioka (三崎東岡) the road to the house is downhill, so going back to that stop is uphill. The last stop, Misaki Port (三崎港), is far from the house.

More →

Can I come by car? Is there parking?

The house has no parking. Please book a nearby parking lot yourself (you pay the fee). Spaces are scarce on holiday weekends. To be confirmed

More →

If I arrive after 19:00, can I still get dinner?

Misaki closes early, and Kurobatei's last order is 19:00. It is safest to buy food at Misakiguchi Station before the bus, or at the nearby convenience store (FamilyMart, 6 min walk), and eat in the kitchen.

As soon as you know, contact the house manager from your booking details page on ADDress. To be confirmed

Can two people stay?

Every private room is for up to 2 people (ADDress official house page).

What should I bring?

A towel, a bag for your valuables, slippers and a jacket in winter, and earplugs if you are sensitive to sound. See “What to bring & seasons”.

Rooms & bedding

Can I lock my room? What about valuables?

You can lock your room from the inside, but it cannot be locked from the outside (no room has an outside lock). Take your valuables with you when you go out.

More →

Can I hear the next room?

This is an old Japanese house, so you can hear the next room. Room 201 in particular is separated by a single fusuma door. Earplugs help if you are sensitive to sound.

More →

How are the rooms different?

Rooms 101 and 203 are Western-style (double bed, desk and chair). Room 201 is Japanese-style (2 futons, low table).

More →

Is it cold in winter? Bugs in summer?

In winter the hallway and kitchen get cold, so slippers and a jacket help. In summer there are mosquitoes; please use the mosquito spray in the house.

More →

Air conditioning and heating?

In summer, the kitchen fan and the air conditioner in the shared washitsu are left on. In winter, the heating is the air conditioner.

More →

I need more bedding

If you need extra bedding, please contact the house manager.

If something happens

The key box won't open

Check the code on the “Entry information” page in the email from the day before. If it still won't open, contact the house manager, then the OpenChat.

More →

There's no hot water

First, check that the water heater switch is on. If it still doesn't work at night, don't force it; contact the house manager.

More →

I feel sick or I'm hurt

If it is life-threatening, call 119. If you are not sure, call #7119 (24 hours, Japanese only).

More →

Earthquake or tsunami?

When it shakes, protect yourself first. If there is a risk of tsunami, go to high ground at once. The nearest is Misaki Elementary School (三崎小学校), about 2–5 minutes' walk. Do not use a car.

More →

My phone alarm went off in the night

Earthquake early warnings may sound in the middle of the night. Stay calm and protect yourself.
