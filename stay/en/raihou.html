MIURA · STAY GUIDE

Visitors, nights away & mail

Rules for inviting friends, staying elsewhere overnight, and receiving parcels.

Inviting friends

  • You need the host's OK in advance.
  • Visitors are allowed from 9:00 to 21:00.
  • Visitors must stay with a member or the host.
  • Other members' use comes first. Visitors cannot use the bath or shower.
  • Visitors may look at rooms that are not booked, but cannot relax or sleep in them.
  • If there is any trouble, the person who invited the visitor is responsible.

Staying elsewhere / daytime-only use

Bookings are for overnight stays. Please do not use the house only during the day or stay elsewhere overnight. If you must stay elsewhere, tell the host. If you already know there are nights you can't stay, please split your booking.

Arriving early / staying late

If you want to drop off your luggage early, or work in the shared spaces after check-out, you need the host's OK in advance. The answer may be no. Only the shared spaces can be used, not the private rooms.

Mail and parcels

  • Please receive them yourself. Parcels that cannot be received are sent back.
  • If you want to send something before you arrive, ask the host first. The answer may be no. Parcels without a delivery date/time, and parcels that need refrigeration, cannot be sent in advance.
  • Neither the operator nor the host can take responsibility for damaged or lost mail or parcels.
  • Address: the address is in the email from the day before. Add “ADDress 三浦A邸” to the recipient name. To be confirmed

Source: ADDress official help (inviting friends / daytime-only use and nights away / using shared spaces before check-in or after check-out / mail and parcels; checked on 2026-11-08).

Meeting the host and regulars

If you would like to meet the host or regular guests, say “交流希望” (I'd like to meet people) in the pre-arrival email after booking. If you don't, you can relax on your own.

Miura A-tei (ADDress). The address, key-box code, and Wi-Fi password are in the email you receive the day before your stay.
